AI Recommendation Monitor · Bolt in Warsaw
🟡 Worth attention

Bolt leads everyday rides. It is rarely the first choice for late-night safety.

Everyday rides
🟢 No immediate action needed — price and availability support Bolt.
Bolt was first choice in 39 of 54 answers.
Bolt is recommended much less often in Polish than in English or Ukrainian.

Getting from Chopin Airport
🟡 Worth attention — GPT and Gemini usually choose Uber; Claude chooses Bolt. Claims about airport access conflict.
Uber was first choice in 35 of 54 answers.

Riding alone late at night
🟡 Worth attention — competitors are recommended for safety tools and professional drivers. This does not prove safer rides.
Bolt was first choice in 5 of 54 answers.

Bolt’s official pages
🟡 Worth attention — search returned Uber pages more often than Bolt pages. This does not prove why AI chose Uber.
Uber pages appeared with 41 answers; Bolt pages with 22.

What to do
Focus the marketing review on airport travel and late-night safety.
1. Ask marketing and product teams to check the claims together: is this a real service gap or missing information?
2. If AI misses a verified Bolt strength, review how official pages and other returned sources describe it.
3. Send service gaps to product and information gaps to content. Retest after updates.
