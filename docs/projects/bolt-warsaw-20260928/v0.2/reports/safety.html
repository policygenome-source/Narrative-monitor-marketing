Why these results?

Uber tools and taxi-driver claims compete for the safety recommendation

GPT focuses on PIN verification and trip sharing. Gemini focuses on licensed, professional taxi drivers. Neither proves that rides are safer.

See supporting answers

GPT-5.6 Sol · English

- **PIN verification** helps ensure you enter the correct car. - **Live trip sharing** lets a trusted contact follow your route.

Gemini 3.8 Flash · English

* **Strictly Licensed Professionals:** Unlike gig-economy platforms that primarily rely on independent private contractors, iTaxi is a network of fully licensed, vetted Polish taxi drivers (including the legacy municipal fleet, MPT).

Bolt sometimes wins on shorter waits, rather than distinctive safety tools

One Claude answer leans toward Bolt because shorter outdoor waits matter at night. It describes Bolt and Uber safety features as comparable.
