AI Recommendation Monitor · Bolt in Warsaw

Bolt leads everyday rides overall. Airport travel, solo night rides and Polish everyday answers need attention.

Polish everyday answers🟡 Worth attention. Bolt is recommended much less often in Polish than in English or Ukrainian.Polish: 7 of 18. English: 17 of 18. Ukrainian: 15 of 18.

WAW Airport Ride🟡 Worth attention. Uber leads airport recommendations. Claims about pickup locations and airport partnerships conflict.Uber was first choice in 35 of 54 answers.

Solo night rides🟡 Worth attention. Bolt is rarely the first choice. Competitors are recommended for safety tools or taxi options.Bolt was first choice in 5 of 54 answers.
